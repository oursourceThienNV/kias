## 🎨 **VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM**

### **Color Palette**
**Primary Colors:**
- **White/Off-white**: Clean, minimalist background
- **Black**: Text, navigation, premium feel
- **Neutral Grays**: Subtle backgrounds and borders

**Accent Colors:**
- **Soft pastels**: Product variants (Jean, Kem, Nâu, Đen, Trắng)
- **Muted tones**: Professional, sophisticated palette
- **No bright/vibrant colors**: Maintains premium, understated aesthetic

### **Typography**
**Characteristics:**
- **Clean, modern sans-serif** fonts
- **Vietnamese language support** (full Unicode)
- **Hierarchy**: Clear H1, H2, H3 structure
- **Readable sizes**: Good contrast and spacing

**Text Styles:**
- **Headers**: Bold, uppercase for sections ("ĐƯỢC YÊU THÍCH NHẤT")
- **Product Names**: Mixed case, descriptive
- **Prices**: Clear pricing with strikethrough for discounts
- **Navigation**: Simple, clean menu items

### **Layout & Spacing**
**Grid System:**
- **Clean, organized layout** with consistent spacing
- **Card-based design** for products
- **Generous white space** - premium feel
- **Responsive design** - works on all devices

**Component Structure:**
- **Header**: Logo center, navigation left, account/cart right
- **Hero Sections**: Large images with overlay text
- **Product Grids**: Consistent card layout
- **Footer**: Comprehensive links and information
